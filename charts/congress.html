Congress copy-trade

Every stock purchase that House members filed between Jul 17 and Oct 15, 2026 (120 periodic transaction reports from the House Clerk, parsed line by line), cut to the 27 tickers with at least $15k bought or a Pelosi trade. Each ticker gets our TA (EMA trend, 6-month fibs, trapped $) and a call. Prices are the daily close on Oct 15, 2026. 4 BUY · 12 WAIT · 11 AVOID.

Read the edge right. Members can report up to 45 days after the trade, so you're buying 2–8 weeks behind them. Amounts are disclosed as ranges; the totals here use the low end. The copy signal is the size and conviction (Pelosi's $3M+ in Bloom Energy shares plus June-2027 $100 calls, 7 members in MSFT), not the date. Never chase. Enter only in the zone.
Rules: BUY = price above 50 & 200 EMA, within 5% of the 20 EMA, RSI under 68. WAIT = good name but extended or trend mixed, so set a limit order in the zone. AVOID = under a falling 200 EMA, with overhead bags (trapped $) that will sell into any bounce. Stops sit 1.5 ATR under the zone or the .618. T1 = 6-month high or 2R; T2 = 1.272 extension or 3.5R.
Schwab IRA: every name here is a US-listed share you can buy in a cash IRA. There's no shorting and no margin. Long calls (Pelosi-style) need options approval in the IRA.
