Mcap-ATH price = all-time-high market cap ÷ today's circulating supply (where price would be if the coin got back to its old market-cap high; far below price ATH when supply has inflated, e.g. ICP).

Moon = how crushed vs ATH + recovery room to mcap-ATH price + float (low float/unlock overhang penalized) + volume ignition (7d vs 30d volume).
Call: Buy zone = trend ≥50, R:R ≥2 and price inside the entry zone · Wait = setup forming or price stretched above the zone · Skip = weak trend or R:R under 1.2.

Floor = % above the 7d/30d/1y lows; "near floor" = within 10% of all three and support intact. Tag: Consensus = on 3+ independent lists (Uphold basket, ETF, Coinbase+Uphold+Binance, top composite); Tribal = 1 or fewer. Filters combine (AND).
Supporting: Technical = price vs 21/55-day EMA, fresh reclaim, near 30-day high, higher lows. Accum = 7-day vs 30-day volume plus up-day vs down-day volume. Supply = market cap ÷ fully diluted value (100 = fully unlocked). Instit. = US spot ETF live (60) or filed (30), Coinbase USD listing (25), top-20/50 rank. Recovery = x to mcap-ATH price on a log scale (1x = 0, 20x+ = 100). Composite = average of every score incl. insider (insider mapped 50 + 10×score).
